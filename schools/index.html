A LITTLE STRUCTURE. LOTS OF CREATIVITY.

Imagine → Build → Try → Share

Plan a small idea, build it, test it, then show a teacher or parent what changed. Leave with a project—not just a high score.

Suggested flow; exact session times are still to be confirmed. Schools and families should plan breaks around their day.

What will they use?

Roblox Studio: a free Windows and Mac app for making Roblox games.

Official setup guide ↗

GROWN-UPS HAVE A PART TO PLAY

Creative time, with support.

Plan for a teacher or parent to supervise, help with setup, and agree on breaks and sharing. Organizer-led supervision hasn’t been confirmed.

A useful conversation afterward

  • “What did you make?”
  • “What was tricky?”
  • “What would you try next?”

For schools, a project and a short reflection can show students’ design choices and revisions.

READY TO HELP THEM GET STARTED?

Start with a little planning.

Check your computer, choose a supervising adult, and let your child or class pick one small idea.
